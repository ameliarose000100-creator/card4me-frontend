How Referral Rewards Work

Your referral reward is handled automatically by CARD4ME.

  • Share your personal CARD4ME referral link.
  • Your invited user creates a CARD4ME account using your link.
  • The invited user must successfully fund their wallet with at least ₦500.
  • Once the qualifying funding is completed, the referrer receives the applicable referral reward.
  • Each referred user can qualify only once.
